What QR Code Ordering delivers

Scan. Order. Pay. Done.

A seamless table ordering experience that benefits customers, staff, and your business.

Faster table service

Customers order as soon as they sit down — no waiting for staff, no queuing at the counter. Tables turn faster and customers enjoy the experience more.

Staff focus on hospitality

When customers order themselves, your team can focus on delivering great food and service — not taking orders and running backwards and forwards between tables.

Fewer order errors

Customers enter their own order — exactly as they want it. Dietary requirements, modifications, and special requests are captured accurately every time.

Direct POS integration

QR orders flow directly to QJumper POS and the kitchen display — the same stream as counter and every other ordering channel. No middleware, no manual relay.

Pay at the table

Customers pay directly through the QR experience — card or mobile wallet. No waiting for the bill. Faster checkout, and one fewer bottleneck at close of service.

No app download needed

The entire ordering experience runs in the customer's mobile browser. Scan, order, pay — no app store, no account required unless you want loyalty integration.

The experience

Four steps for your customers

1

Scan the QR code

A QR code on the table, tent card, or receipt opens your branded ordering experience instantly in their browser.

2

Browse the menu

Your full digital menu — with photography, descriptions, and allergen information — right on their phone.

3

Place the order

Add items, customise as needed, and confirm. The order fires directly to the kitchen.

4

Pay and leave

Card or mobile wallet payment on their own device. No waiting for the bill. Done.
