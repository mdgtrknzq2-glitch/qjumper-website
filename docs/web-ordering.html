What QJumper Web Ordering delivers

Online ordering you own completely

A direct-to-kitchen online ordering channel that represents your brand and keeps your revenue intact.

Your brand, your storefront

Fully branded to your venue — your colours, your logo, your photography. Customers ordering online feel like they're interacting with your business, not a generic ordering platform.

Commission-free

No percentage taken on every order. You set your prices, you take the revenue. Web ordering through QJumper pays for itself fast when compared to third-party platform fees.

Direct POS integration

Web orders fire directly into QJumper POS — exactly like a counter order. No manual relay, no parallel system, no end-of-day reconciliation between platforms.

Menu management in one place

Update your menu once in QJumper and it reflects across web ordering, kiosk, POS, and QR — simultaneously. No separate admin panel or double-handling of menu changes.

Seamless checkout

Card and mobile wallet payments built in. A clean, fast checkout flow that doesn't frustrate customers or lead to abandoned carts before they place the order.

Unified reporting

Web orders are captured alongside every other channel in QJumper reporting. One dashboard shows your full picture — no manual import from a separate platform.

Own every channel your customers use

Web ordering is even more powerful when connected to your kiosks, mobile app, and QR table ordering — all in one platform.
