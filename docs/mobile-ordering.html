What Mobile Ordering delivers

More than an ordering app

A direct customer relationship channel that drives loyalty, repeat visits, and higher lifetime value.

Loyalty that actually works

Points, stamps, rewards, and tier programmes built into the app. Customers earn with every order and come back to redeem — creating a habit that drives consistent repeat revenue.

Push notifications

Reach your customers directly on their phone — promotions, new menu items, event alerts. No paid advertising. No algorithm. Direct communication with your most valuable customers.

Fully branded

Your name in the App Store and Google Play. Your colours, your logo, your photography. Customers experience your brand — not generic ordering software.

Reorder in seconds

Customers who order regularly can reorder their favourites with a couple of taps. The lower the friction, the more often they come back.

Connected to your POS

Mobile orders fire directly into QJumper POS — the same stream as counter, kiosk, and web orders. No separate system, no manual relay, no end-of-day headaches.

Customer behaviour insights

See what your loyal customers order, when they visit, and how they respond to promotions. Data that helps you run a smarter, more profitable business.

The app your customers will actually use

Mobile Ordering works alongside every other QJumper channel — so whether customers order from the app, the kiosk, or the counter, loyalty points and data flow into one unified system.
